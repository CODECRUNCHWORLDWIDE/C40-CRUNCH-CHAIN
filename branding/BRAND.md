# C40 · Crunch Chain — Brand Guide

- **Accent:** Code Crunch blue `#2463EB` (with deep `#1d4ed8`).
- **Voice:** operational and evidence-first. Always show the number, then the decision it drives, then the cost of getting it wrong. Nothing runs on gut feel — a supply chain is a system you can measure, model, and optimize. Records live in SQL, analysis runs in Python; never a spreadsheet.
- **Icon motif:** linked nodes on a flow — supplier → warehouse → customer — with a trend line running through them.
- **One-liner:** "Run the chain on numbers — forecast, optimize, and automate operations end to end."
