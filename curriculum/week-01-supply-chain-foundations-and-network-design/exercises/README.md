# Week 1 — Exercises

Three guided exercises, ~1–1.5 hours each. All three are worked with pencil, paper, and a calculator — no software required. **Do the arithmetic yourself** — don't skip to a calculator app for every step; the point is to build a felt sense of what these numbers mean, so that when you compute them from real data starting Week 2, the SQL result is a number you can sanity-check instantly.

1. **[Exercise 1 — Map a real chain end to end](exercise-01-map-a-real-chain.md)** — pick a real product and trace its nodes, echelons, and three flows.
2. **[Exercise 2 — Compute core KPIs by hand](exercise-02-compute-core-kpis-by-hand.md)** — OTIF, fill rate, perfect order, inventory turns, and cash-to-cash from a 10-order table.
3. **[Exercise 3 — Sketch two network designs](exercise-03-sketch-two-network-designs.md)** — design and compare a centralized vs. distributed network for a growing DTC brand.

## Before you start

- You've read all three lectures.
- You have paper (or a blank document) and a calculator handy.
- You've skimmed the Crunch Gear example from the lectures — you'll reuse its vocabulary and, in Exercise 2, its KPI formulas against new numbers.

## Suggested workflow

- Attempt each task **before** checking the "Expected" hint — struggling for 60 seconds on a formula is where the learning happens.
- Show your work. For the KPI exercise especially, write out numerator and denominator explicitly — it's the fastest way to catch your own arithmetic slip before it becomes a wrong SQL query in Week 2.
- If a result surprises you (e.g., a KPI that looks worse than you expected), stop and figure out *why* before moving on. That's usually the lesson.
