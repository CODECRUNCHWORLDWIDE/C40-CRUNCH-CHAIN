# Week 1 — Challenges

Two open-ended problems. Unlike the exercises, these have **no single right answer** — the skill being tested is judgment and clear reasoning, not recall of a formula. Do them after the three exercises.

1. **[Challenge 1 — Diagnose a broken KPI](challenge-01-diagnose-a-broken-kpi.md)** — a dashboard looks fine except one number. Find the real problem underneath the metric, not just the metric itself. *(~60 min.)*
2. **[Challenge 2 — Argue centralize vs. distribute](challenge-02-argue-centralize-vs-distribute.md)** — write and defend a network recommendation for a real-feeling scenario, with numbers. *(~60 min.)*

## How these are judged

There's no answer key with one correct number. Each challenge tells you what a *strong* submission looks like. You're being graded on:

- **Correctness of reasoning** — does your conclusion actually follow from the data you're given?
- **Explicit assumptions** — did you state the judgment calls you made, instead of hiding them inside a number?
- **Use of this week's frameworks** — did you actually reach for OTIF/fill-rate/perfect-order composition (Challenge 1) and the square-root-law/cost-service curve (Challenge 2), or just give an opinion?

Keep your work in `challenge-01.md` / `challenge-02.md` with your numbers **and** your written reasoning. The reasoning is the point.
