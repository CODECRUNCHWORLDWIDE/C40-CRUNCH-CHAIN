# Week 6 Challenges

Two challenges. Both are open-ended and stretch past the exercises — there's a defensible right *range* of answers, not one right number, and both require you to state your assumptions explicitly.

1. **[Challenge 1 — Supplier Award Allocation](./challenge-01-supplier-award-allocation.md)** — split next quarter's CMT volume across three capacity-constrained suppliers, balancing TCO against concentration risk.
2. **[Challenge 2 — Build a Should-Cost Model](./challenge-02-should-cost-model.md)** — build a bottom-up should-cost estimate from labor, overhead, and margin, and find the real negotiation gap.

## Before you start

- Complete all three lectures and all three exercises — both challenges reuse the scorecard and TCO methods from Lectures 2–3 without re-deriving them.
- Both challenges expect **written justification**, not just a number. A correct allocation percentage or should-cost figure with no reasoning attached is an incomplete answer.
- No solutions are published here — check your reasoning against the "what a strong answer covers" checklist at the end of each challenge.
